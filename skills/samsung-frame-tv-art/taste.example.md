# Taste (lives at ~/.config/frame-tv/taste.md; the agent writes and updates it)

## What the TV should show
- For whom, and what each person loves: e.g. "Me: Japan, anime, JRPGs. My partner: the Silk Road and Anatolia, no China."
- Split between people/regions: e.g. about 50/50.
- Mood: calm, beautiful. Avoid: text, logos, crowds, close-up faces, cars and wires.

## Decisions
- Approval: e.g. "approves the type sheet only" / "wants to review every new batch" / "no approval needed, just tell me what was added".
- Rejected types and why: e.g. "old maps: render badly full-screen".
- Own photos on the TV: how many, and whether they stay in the shuffle (2025 Frames can't isolate favourites over the API).
- Shuffle interval: e.g. 15 min.

## The TV
- Model and size, e.g. 65" 2025 LS03F.
